I'm Sourav, a software engineer who never got over the fact that we live on a pale blue dot. Someone has to write the software that makes sense of it.

By day I build systems and pipelines, the unglamorous plumbing that moves data where it needs to go, fast, without dropping it. By night I take things apart to see what they're made of: codebases, arguments, occasionally an actual gadget.
